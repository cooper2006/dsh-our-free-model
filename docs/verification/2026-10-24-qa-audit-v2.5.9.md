**严重程度**: P1（阻塞发布）

**严重程度**: P3（低优先级）  
**问题**: 以下测试脚本不在自动化测试套件中：`channel-pr-fixes-test.mjs`, `channel-storage-compat-test.mjs`, `headroom-test.mjs`, `image-pricing-host-test.mjs`, `managed-upgrade-ui-test.mjs`, `qoder-effort-order-test.mjs`, `sponsor-ui-test.mjs`, `standalone-login-ui-test.mjs`, `standalone-model-row-test.mjs`, `standalone-react-ui-test.mjs`
